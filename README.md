# APSS Research Website & Main Site Redesign

**ENSE 271: People-Centred Design · University of Regina · Fall 2026 · Group B**

A people-centred design project for **AIDS Programs South Saskatchewan (APSS)**. We are designing a new research website for APSS and proposing a redesign of its main website. Both will be built in WordPress.

## About the client

APSS is a community-based charity in Regina. It supports people living with or affected by HIV/AIDS and hepatitis C, and people at risk of infection or overdose, through education, prevention, testing, harm reduction, needle exchange and support services.

## The problem

APSS has worked with the University of Regina on research projects connected to [reportneedles.ca](https://reportneedles.ca), its tool for reporting and mapping discarded needles. That research has no home of its own: there is no single place to find the projects, publications, posters and updates.

## What we are building

1. **Research website (MVP).** A dedicated, searchable site where visitors can:
   - browse current and completed research projects
   - read journal publications and articles
   - view research posters
   - follow research updates through a blog
   - move easily between the research site, the main APSS site and reportneedles.ca
2. **Main website redesign (proposal).** A clearer main site where needle exchange and support services are one click away, icons lead somewhere useful, and AI-generated artwork is replaced.

## Who we are designing for

- **North Star customer:** people affected by HIV/AIDS, hepatitis C and other infectious diseases who rely on APSS for harm reduction, needle exchange and support groups.
- **Carryover customers:** the general public, including donors and supporters, people learning about these diseases and their history, people requesting needle clean-up, and the researchers, students and partners who use APSS research.
