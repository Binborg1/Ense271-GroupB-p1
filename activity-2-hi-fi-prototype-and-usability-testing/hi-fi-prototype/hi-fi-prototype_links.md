The links above hide Figma's toolbar (`hide-ui=1`) so it doesn't cover the site's top bar, and turn off hotspot hints (`hotspot-hints=0`). Press **R** to restart a flow.

You can also open the file, go to the **Desktop prototype** or **Mobile prototype** page and press **Present**. If Figma's toolbar covers the top of the screen there, turn off **Options › Show Figma UI**.
