Before we start

We’re testing the design, not you

1

Think aloud as you go, and say “done” when you finish.

2

You can click but not type. Clicking a search box shows sample results.

3

Links that leave APSS open a grey stand-in page.

4

After each task, rate it from 1 (very hard) to 5 (very easy).

5

If something is hard, that’s ours to fix. There are no wrong answers.

6

We take notes only. Nothing is recorded on video or audio.
